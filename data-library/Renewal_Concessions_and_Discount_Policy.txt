RENEWAL CONCESSIONS AND DISCOUNT POLICY - FY27
Owner: Revenue Operations and Finance.

1. APPROVAL LEVELS FOR RENEWAL DISCOUNTS
Up to 5 percent off the renewal amount: CSM Manager approval.
More than 5 and up to 10 percent: VP of Customer Success approval.
More than 10 percent: CFO approval, requested through the Deal Desk.
Discounts are calculated against the current annual contract value, not list price.

2. SERVICE CREDITS FOR ESCALATED CASES
When a customer has an escalated case open at renewal time, the CSM may offer a one-time service credit instead of a discount. Service credits are capped at 10 percent of the annual contract value and need VP of Customer Success approval.

3. LOW UTILIZATION
If seat utilization is under 50 percent, the preferred concession is a rightsizing (fewer seats at the same per-seat price) plus a free adoption workshop. Multi-year discounts are not allowed while utilization is under 50 percent.

4. MULTI-YEAR RENEWALS
A 2-year renewal may include up to 5 percent extra discount; a 3-year renewal up to 8 percent. Both need VP of Customer Success approval.

5. WHAT NOT TO OFFER
Do not offer free months, uncapped service credits, or price locks beyond 3 years.
