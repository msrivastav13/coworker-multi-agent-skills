ESCALATION MANAGEMENT STANDARD
Owner: Support Operations.

1. WHAT COUNTS AS ESCALATED
A case is escalated when its Status is Escalated or its Escalated flag is set. Escalated cases on accounts with an open renewal are reported to Customer Success every day.

2. REQUIRED FOR EVERY ESCALATED CASE
2.1 A named executive owner from Support or Engineering within 1 business day.
2.2 A customer update every business day until the case is resolved.
2.3 A target resolution date shared with the customer.
2.4 A root-cause analysis (RCA) delivered within 5 business days of resolution.

3. HIGH PRIORITY CASES
High priority cases that are not escalated need a customer update every 2 business days. If a High priority case is open for more than 10 business days, Support escalates it.

4. RENEWAL INTERACTION
Before an executive sponsor call for a High risk renewal, the CSM must have the current status, executive owner, and target date of every escalated case on the account.
